    What's a baby guess pool?

    It's the oldest baby-shower game there is, minus the paper slips. Everyone guesses two things — the day the baby arrives and how much they'll weigh — and once the little one turns up, whoever guessed closest wins. No prize required; the bragging rights carry the whole thing. Some people call it a baby pool, a baby sweepstake, or a guess-the-date game. Same idea, and this is the free online version.

    How it works

    1. Start the pool

    Pop in the parents' names and, if you like, a rough due date and a note. You get two links: a share link for the group chat or the shower invite, and an organiser link that records the birth and can tidy up guesses. Bookmark the organiser one — there are no accounts, so the link is the only key.

    2. Everyone guesses

    Guests tap the share link, put in their name, a date, and a weight in kilograms (type "3.4" — it's stored to the gram under the hood), plus an optional cheeky message. No app, no login. Their browser remembers their own guesses so they can take one back if they change their mind.

    3. Record the arrival

    When bub arrives, open your organiser link and enter the actual date and weight (and the time, if you're feeling thorough). That closes guessing and reveals the leaderboard to everyone — winner up top, sorted by who came closest.

    How the winner is decided

    The scoring is deliberately simple and shown right on the page, so nobody can accuse the organiser of fudging it. Each guess earns a closeness score:

    • One point for every day away from the actual arrival date.
    • One point for every 100 grams away from the actual weight.

    Add them up; the lowest score wins. That means being 200 grams out counts about the same as being two days out — date and weight pull roughly equal weight, so you can't just spam a guess right on the due date and ignore the scales. If two guesses tie, the one made first takes it.

    FAQ

    Is it actually free?

    Yes. No account, no trial, no card. It's a small tool, not a platform, and it never touches money.

    How is the winner decided?

    By a combined closeness score: one point per day off the real date, plus one point per 100 grams off the real weight. Lowest score wins, ties go to whoever guessed first. The rule is printed on the pool so it's all in the open.

    Can guests guess without an account?

    Yes — that's the whole point. They tap the link, type a name, a date and a weight, and they're in. Their browser quietly remembers which guesses are theirs so they can pull one back.

    Can I run it for a baby shower before the birth?

    Absolutely — that's the classic use. Start the pool, share the link on the shower invite or in the group chat, and collect guesses for as long as you like. You only record the actual arrival weeks later, whenever bub decides to show up.

    Does it handle prizes or money?

    No, deliberately. It sorts out who guessed closest; if there's a prize, that stays between you and the group, same as always.

    Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

    The pool runs in kilograms — the Australian default. If your guests think in pounds, they can convert (1 lb is about 0.45 kg) and enter the kilogram figure. The scoring works the same either way.
