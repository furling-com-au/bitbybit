      A group greeting card, signed from a link

      This is an online group greeting card — the kind everyone in an office or a class signs before it gets handed over. Farewells and birthdays mostly, but also get-well, congratulations, thank-yous, and the card for the teacher at the end of the year.

      The difference from a paper card is that nobody has to be in the building. The difference from most online ones is that there is no account, no per-card fee, and no cap on how many people sign.

      How it works

      1. Start the card

      Say who it's for, add a line at the top if you like — when to sign by, what the occasion is, whether it's a surprise. Ten seconds, no account.

      2. Share the link — carefully

      You get two links: a share link for everyone who's signing, and an organiser link that can remove messages or delete the card. Bookmark the organiser one — there are no accounts, so the link is the only key. Anyone with the share link types a name and a message, done. Two Sams? Both fine — nobody's forced to become Sam H.

      3. Hand it over

      When the card's full, send the recipient the same share link so they can read the lot — or print it. The print view is just the messages, no buttons, ready for an actual envelope if you're feeling traditional.

      One rule: don't let them see it early

      The share link is the card, so the classic blunder is posting it in a group chat the recipient is in. Make a side chat without them — every workplace farewell already runs on one — and drop the link there. The organiser page repeats this warning, because someone ignores it every week and we'd rather it wasn't you.

      Stuck on what to actually write? There's a long list of group card message ideas — farewell lines for a colleague or a boss, retirement, new baby, big birthday, get well and sympathy, plus the hard one nobody covers: what to write when you barely know them.

      FAQ

      Is it actually free?

      Yes. The big group-card sites charge per card once you pass a handful of messages, or want you on a subscription. This one's a small tool, not a platform: no account, no trial, no card required to make a card.

      Do people need accounts to sign?

      No — open the link, type a name and a message, done. It works on whatever phone the link lands on.

      Can a message be removed?

      Yes, from either end. Signers can take their own message back off (same browser they signed from), and the organiser link can remove any message — handy when someone's "joke" doesn't survive contact with daylight.

      How do we give it to them?

      Share the link with the recipient once everyone's signed, or print the page — the print view is just the card. Either way it stays up afterwards, unless the organiser deletes it.
