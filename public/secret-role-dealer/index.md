## For hidden-role party games

Werewolf and Mafia, Spyfall, Avalon, Resistance, Insider, Bang — the **party games** where everyone gets a secret role and the whole point is that nobody knows who has what. The awkward part has always been the dealing: a deck to shuffle, cards to hand round face-down, and someone catching a glimpse.

Type the roles you want, share one link, and each player draws privately on their own phone. It works for a printed game you have lost the cards for, a variant you made up, or a class exercise that needs secret assignments.

## How it works

### 1. Make the deal

Type the roles, one per line — or hit a preset and it writes a standard setup for your player count. Duplicates are normal: a ten-player Werewolf game is mostly a stack of Villagers. Anything you can type is a role, so it works for any hidden-role game, not just the famous ones.

### 2. Share one link

You get a share link for the group chat and an organiser link for you. Everyone opens the same share link — no app to install, no accounts, works on whatever phone is already in their hand.

### 3. Each player takes a role privately

A player types their name, taps *deal me in*, and lands on a private page showing only their role. The draw is random and atomic — two people tapping at once can't get the same slot, and nobody can see anyone else's page.

## FAQ

### Can players see each other's roles?

No. Each player gets their own private page at a secret link, and the shared link never shows a role to anyone. The only way to leak your role is the time-honoured one: waving your screen around.

### Can the organiser play too?

Yes — that's the point of dealing at random. The organiser page lists who's joined but hides the roles, so you can join from the share link like everyone else. There's a reveal-all link for after the game, clearly labelled as the spoiler it is.

### What if two people have the same name?

Names must be unique within a game, so the second Sam gets asked to add a surname initial. Better than two people arguing over one Seer.

### Can I re-deal?

The organiser can reset a single player (their role goes back in the pool) or re-deal everything — that clears every player and every private link, and the group joins again from scratch with a fresh shuffle.

### Is it actually free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no card. It's a small tool, not a platform.
