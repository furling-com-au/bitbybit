# Wishing well wording

Asking without the awkward

You'd rather have help with the house deposit than a fourth toaster, and your guests would rather give you something you actually want. The only hard part is the sentence in between. Here it is, several ways — copy whichever sounds like you.

## Short and modern

Your presence is truly the best gift we could ask for.
 But if you'd like to give something more, a contribution to our

[honeymoon / first home / big lap]

would mean the world.

No gifts, please — but if you insist (and we know some
 of you will), a wishing well will be at the reception.

We've lived together long enough to own two of
 everything. If you'd like to give a gift, a little towards

[the thing]

would make us very happy.

## The traditional verse

More important than gifts to unwrap,

is having you here on our special day.

But if a gift is your wish, a wishing well we will display.

A note inside would be divine — just having you here is the best gift of all.

Rhyming verses read sweetly on letterpress invitations and terribly in text messages. Match the format to the medium.

## Funny, if that's your crowd

We have enough candles to survive several blackouts.
 Help us buy

[the thing]

instead.

Gifts are optional. Dancing is not.

## The three rules of asking

- **Lead with presence, not presents.** Every good wording makes clear that turning up is the gift.
- **Name the thing.** "A contribution to our camping rig" lands warmer than "cash preferred". People give to a story, not to a balance.
- **Never name amounts.** The well is anonymous by design; that's what makes it comfortable.

## Or skip the well entirely

The wishing well's weakness is that it's a box. Nobody knows what their gift became, and you write forty thank-you notes for "your generous contribution".

The [pixel gift registry](https://bitibybit.com/via/registry) flips that: you show the thing you're saving for as a pixel drawing — a 4WD, say — and guests claim an actual part of it. Nan buys the spare tyre, your mates split the bull bar, someone gets the $15 air freshener, and the picture paints itself in as it's funded. Every thank-you note names the part. It's free, guests pay you directly by PayID or transfer, and nobody takes a cut.
