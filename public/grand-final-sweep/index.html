    How it works

    1. Set it up

    Type the two teams and the tool writes the classic margin outcomes for you — six margin bands per team. Edit them however you like: fewer bands, a "match drawn" line, first-goal-scorer names, anything. One outcome per line.

    2. Draw

    Paste everyone's names and hit draw. The shuffle uses your browser's cryptographic randomness — nobody can rig it, including us. Every outcome gets a name: if there are fewer people than outcomes, some lucky people draw twice; if there are more people than outcomes, the surplus miss out and get listed, so add more outcomes to fit everyone in.

    3. Share the link

    You get two links: a share link that shows everyone the drawn board, and an organiser link that lets you re-draw or delete. Bookmark the organiser one — there are no accounts, so the link is the only key. The shared page prints cleanly for the tea-room fridge.

    FAQ

    Is it actually free?

    Yes. No account, no trial, no card. It's a small tool, not a platform.

    Does the sweep handle money?

    No — deliberately. Collect the gold coins however your office always has. This just does the drawing and the remembering.

    What margins should we use?

    The default is six bands per team (1–9 through 60+), which suits 10–14 people. For a bigger group, split the bands finer; for a smaller one, use 1–24, 25–49, 50+ per team.

    Can I re-draw?

    The organiser link can re-draw the whole board any time before the game. Everyone's assignments reshuffle, and the shared link updates.
